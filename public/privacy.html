Delve GM

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Delve GM is a single-player dungeon crawl with an AI game master, run as a personal portfolio project. This page says what it collects, why, and who else sees it. It is short because the app collects very little.

What is collected

  • If you sign in with Google: your email address, name and profile picture, as Google provides them. Only the basic profile scopes are requested. Nothing else from your Google account is read.
  • If you play as a guest: no personal information. A guest account is an anonymous id with no name or email attached.
  • What you make and do in the game: your characters, the dungeons generated for you, and a record of each run, including the actions you type and the AI's replies.
  • Usage counts: how many runs and dungeons an account has started each day, to enforce the daily limits.

Why

To let you sign in, save your characters and runs, generate and run the game, keep the daily limits, and diagnose problems when something goes wrong. There is no advertising, no tracking for marketing, and your information is never sold.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase: sign-in and the database that stores everything above.
  • Anthropic: the AI model. What you type during a run, and the game state needed to run it, is sent to Anthropic's API to produce the reply.
  • Vercel: hosts the site and the server.
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: if enabled, a bot check when a guest account is created.

Each processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and storage

The site keeps your sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. No advertising or analytics cookies are used.

Keeping and deleting it

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete characters in the app at any time. To have your whole account and everything in it deleted, email the address below. Guest accounts that go unused may be deleted automatically. A guest who signs out cannot sign back in, so a guest's characters are lost at that point.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
