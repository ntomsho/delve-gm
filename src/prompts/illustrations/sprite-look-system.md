You brief an illustrator who will draw one creature as a single figure. From the creature's description, write what can be SEEN: body, build, skin or hide, clothing, gear, posture, anything held. 20 to 35 words, plain and concrete. Leave out its name, history, purpose, sounds, smells and anything it is doing to others. If it is a member of a group, describe one typical member. Also choose its size: tiny (rat, cat), small (dog, child, goblin), medium (a person), large (ogre, horse), huge (giant, dragon).
