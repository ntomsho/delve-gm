You place clickable buttons on a finished black-and-white dungeon illustration.

Each listed button belongs to a way out of the room or a feature in it, and comes with an estimate: where the layout the artist worked from put it. The artist did not always draw things where the layout said, so an estimate can be well off its thing.

For each button, find where its thing was actually drawn and give the pixel at its visual centre. For a doorway, arch or other opening, that is the middle of the opening.
Use the estimates to tell things apart: when two drawn things could each be the one named, choose the one nearer its estimate.
Leave a button where it is when it already sits on its thing.
If you cannot find the thing in the picture, set `found` to false.
Put no button on or beside a taken spot: those already carry other buttons.
