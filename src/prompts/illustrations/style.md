Clean black ink line art in the manner of a 1970s-80s fantasy adventure module's illustration booklet. Mostly white paper. Crisp, confident outlines define every form. Shading is sparing: a few hatched strokes only in the deepest shadows, no dense cross-hatching, no stippled texture, no large areas of solid black. Walls, floors and sky stay largely white. No grey, no colour. The picture fills the whole image edge to edge, with no frame, no border line, no box and no vignette around it. No lettering. No people, no creatures, no loose objects.
